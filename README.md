## 🎨 UI/UX Design Principles Implemented

1. **Aesthetic-Usability Effect**:
   - Modern editorial layout pairing *Playfair Display* (luxury serif) with *Plus Jakarta Sans* (crisp grotesque sans-serif) and *JetBrains Mono* (code/tokens).
   - Obsidian dark mode with champagne gold (`#E5B365`) and subtle ambient cursor glow.
   - One-click toggle to a crisp ivory Light Theme (`[data-theme="light"]`).

2. **Fitts's Law & Touch Usability**:
   - High-contrast, ergonomically placed Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons (`Hitung Estimasi Proyek`, `Mulai Proyek`, `Lihat Portofolio`).
   - Generous touch targets (>44px) across mobile drawer menus, pills, and filter buttons.

3. **Hick's Law & Cognitive Load Minimization (Miller's Law)**:
   - Chunked information cards with progressive disclosure.
   - Interactive segmented tabs to prevent information overload.
   - Interactive Scope Estimator with instant visual breakdown.

4. **Jakob's Law & Familiar UX Affordances**:
   - Standard sticky glassmorphic navigation header with scroll progress styling.
   - Intuitive modal dialogs with escape/backdrop close handlers and accessible focus management.

5. **Accessibility & Usability (WCAG AA)**:
   - Semantic HTML5 structure (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
   - ARIA roles, live regions, labels, and color contrast verified.

6. **Bilingual Support (i18n)**:
   - Instant language switching between **Bahasa Indonesia** and **English** without page reload.
